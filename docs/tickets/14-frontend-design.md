# 14 — BahayFind frontend design

**What to build:** The whole site looks like the team's "BahayFind –
Frontend Design" boards: navy and amber colours, the Plus Jakarta Sans
font, a header with the logo and links, a navy hero with the price filter
floating over it, new room cards, split-screen Log in and Sign up pages,
and a navy sidebar on the team's review page. This changes how the app
looks only. What each page does, and who can see what, stays exactly the
same.
