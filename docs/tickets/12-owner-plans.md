# 12 — Owner plans

**What to build:** Boarding house owners have a plan: Free, Premium Owner
or Property Manager. A Free owner can post one room; Premium and Property
Manager owners can post several. A public Plans page shows the three
plans honestly. The team changes a plan by hand. The app takes no
payments, and students are never charged.
