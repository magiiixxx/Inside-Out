# BahayFind — Session 1 Pitch

Speakers: **A** opens and closes, **B** takes the middle. Swap the
labels if you have a different number of presenters.

## The script

**[A — The person]**
Meet Richelle. She is a student from the province, new to Iloilo, and
she needed a room near PHINMA UI. We watched her search. She went
through Facebook groups and messaged more than eight owners, asking the
same two questions every time: how much, and is it still free? Then she
found a room that looked perfect and travelled to see it. When she
arrived, it was already taken. The post was still up.

**[B — Today]**
That trip cost her about nine hundred pesos in fare and more than six
hours. And this is not just Richelle. It happens every enrollment
period, when hundreds of new students arrive at once. Facebook shows
posts. It does not tell you whether a room is still available, or how
old the post is. So students guess, and they pay for the guess.

**[B — Our fix]**
We are building BahayFind. Boarding house owners sign up and post their
own rooms, with the price, the distance from PHINMA UI, their contact,
and one label that matters most: available or occupied. When a room is
taken, the owner changes the label. Our team approves every posting
before a student can see it. A student opens the page, sees what is
really free today, and calls only those owners. No login for students.
No money handled.

**[A — The demo moment]**
In Session 3 we will show you the whole flow. An owner signs up and
posts a room. We approve it. Then a student opens the live page on a
phone, finds a room under their budget, and sees it is available. All
in three minutes.

**[A — How we'll know]**
Before Session 3, three people will try it. Richelle will tell us
whether she would have skipped that nine hundred peso trip. Joshua, a
student, will try to find a room under his budget in under a minute.
And Monica, a boarding house owner, will sign up and post her own room.
Then we will tell you what they said, good or bad.

**[B — How we'll earn]**
Students will always use BahayFind for free. Owners are our customers.
A new owner starts free. An owner with several rooms can upgrade for a
small monthly fee, for example one hundred forty-nine pesos. We have not
proven that owners will pay. That is the next thing we will test.

## The three hardest questions

**1. "You have watched one student. How do you know this is a real problem
for many?"**
This is the weakest point in our evidence. We watched Richelle, and her
story is concrete, but it is one person. Honest answer: "Richelle is our
clearest case. Before Session 3, Joshua and at least two more students
will tell us whether the same thing happened to them." Strengthen it now:
ask five students at PHINMA UI "Have you ever travelled to a room that
was already taken?" and report how many said yes.

**2. "Why would owners keep updating the label? If they don't, it is the
same as Facebook."**
This is the biggest risk in the design, because the whole promise depends
on owners updating their rooms. Honest answer: "Owners want full rooms,
and the label is one tap. We have not proven they will do it, which is
why Monica is testing it. And if an owner does nothing, we notify them,
then hide the room after seven days until they renew it. A stale post
can't stay up the way Richelle's did."

**3. "What does your approval actually check? Why should a student
trust 'approved'?"**
Honest answer: "Before a listing goes live, our team checks the owner's
permit and the owner's answers to a short set of validation questions."
Say exactly that and no more. Do not claim "verified" beyond what you
actually check. Be ready for: "Do owners know their phone numbers will be public?"
Tell owners clearly when they post.

## One more question to expect

**"How will this make money?"**
Honest answer: "Students stay free. Owners are the customers. We have
three draft plans: Free, Premium Owner at ₱149 a month, and Property
Manager at ₱299 a month. These prices are guesses. Before we build any
of it, we will ask real owners like Monica whether they would pay. Today
the app handles no money at all." The full plans are in `docs/LATER.md`.
Do not claim the plans exist in the app, because none of it is built.

## Before you present

- Evidence is thin: one watched student, and the other two testers have
  not tried it yet. Do the five-student question above this week.
- Write down the actual validation questions you will ask owners. A
  facilitator will ask what they are.
- Get at least one real owner (Monica) to agree to post, and say how many
  others you have asked.
- Practise it aloud once with a timer. If it runs over three minutes,
  cut the "Today" beat first.
